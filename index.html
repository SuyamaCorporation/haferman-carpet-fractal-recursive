About Haferman Carpets

The Sierpiński carpet is the special case where the center cell of a 3×3 grid is removed. After n iterations, 8ⁿ filled squares remain out of 9ⁿ total.

The Haferman carpet generalizes this: any subset of cells can be designated as holes. Different patterns produce different fractal dimensions and visual textures.

The fractal (Hausdorff) dimension is:

d = log(filled) / log(3)

For Sierpiński carpet: d = log(8)/log(3) ≈ 1.893

Patterns with more holes have lower dimension; denser patterns approach d=2.
